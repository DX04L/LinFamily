      How to Jump

      Tap for a short hop or hold for more height. On a keyboard, use Space, ↑, or W.

      Tap for a Short Hop

      Release quickly to clear low obstacles.

      Hold for a High Jump

      Hold a little longer to jump higher.

      Tap the arrows ❯❯❯ in the bottom-right corner to pause. Tap the game screen to resume.

      Lift your device gently for a short hop; the bigger and stronger the lift, the higher you jump. Switch back to Controls to use the screen.

      Tap Connect to use this phone 👆

      Put the phone and the game on the same Wi-Fi (or a phone hotspot) for a direct, faster link. 🟢 Direct　🟠 Internet

      Game Guide

      Pickups

      Coin
      Pick it up for 50 points.
      Star
      Pick it up for 100 points.
      Heart
      Gain a heart that absorbs one hit. Hold up to five.

      On the Course

      Peaks
      From 200 points. Jump over them; tall peaks appear after 1,500. You can run straight over rolling terrain.
      Crystals
      From the start. Jump over blue and pink crystals. After 1,500 points they sometimes come in pairs.
      Bird
      From 200 points. Birds fly low. Time your jump to avoid them.
      Plane
      From 350 points. Planes fly toward you in different sizes, getting bigger at higher scores. They fly high, so stay low and run underneath.
      Boost Gate
      From 600 points. Clouds won't hurt you. Jump into the gate to get pulled through the middle with a speed boost and grab the stars: two after 1,500 points, three after 3,000.

      The longer you run, the faster it gets and the closer the obstacles come.

      Characters

      • Change character on the start and game-over screens: tap ◀ ▶, swipe on the character, or press ← → on a computer. Tap the character to read about it.
      • With a paired phone, change character on the phone: tap ◀ ▶ in Phone Control, or tilt the phone left or right in Motion Jump.
      • Every character plays the same for now. After a crash you stay with the same character for Play Again.

      HUD and Buttons

      • 🏆 TOP and HI open the leaderboard. SCORE tracks your current run.
      • ♥ Shows your remaining hearts (up to 5). Tap the paw gear for Settings and the Game Guide.
      • ⛶ at the top right toggles fullscreen (not shown on iPhone, which doesn't support it). 🔊 in Settings toggles sound, and 📲 installs the game on your device. When pairing a phone, the controller also has ⛶ (phone fullscreen) and 🔊 (game sound on/off).
