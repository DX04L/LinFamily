      Settings

      How to Jump

      Tap for a short hop or hold for more height. On a keyboard, use Space, ↑, or W.

      Tap for a Short Hop

      Release quickly to clear low obstacles.

      Hold for a High Jump

      Hold a little longer to jump higher.

      Tap the arrows ❯❯❯ in the bottom-right corner to pause. Tap the game screen to resume.

      Lift your device gently for a short hop, or lift it faster to jump higher. Switch back to Controls to use the screen.

      Tap Connect to use this phone 👆

      Put the phone and the game on the same Wi-Fi (or a phone hotspot) for a direct, faster link. 🟢 Direct　🟠 Internet

      Game Guide

      Pickups

      Coin
      Pick it up for 50 points.
      Star
      Pick it up for 100 points.
      Heart
      Gain a heart that absorbs one hit. Hold up to five.

      On the Course

      Peaks
      Jump over both short and tall peaks. You can run straight over rolling terrain.
      Crystals
      Jump over blue and pink crystals. Sometimes they come in pairs.
      Bird
      Birds fly low. Time your jump to avoid them.
      Plane
      Planes fly high. Stay low and run underneath.
      Cloud Gap
      Clouds won't hurt you. Aim for the star in the gap.

      HUD and Buttons

      • 🏆 TOP and HI open the leaderboard. SCORE tracks your current run.
      • ♥ Shows your remaining hearts (up to 5). Tap the paw gear for Settings and the Game Guide.
      • ⛶ Toggles fullscreen, 🔊 toggles sound, and 📲 installs the game on your device.

      Pinky Jump

      Hold your phone steady to calibrate. Once the run starts, lift it gently to hop or faster to jump higher.
